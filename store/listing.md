# Google Play store listing for Wall Hop

Copy these into Play Console under **Grow > Store presence > Main store listing**.

**App name** (30 characters max)
Wall Hop

**Short description** (80 characters max)
Tap to leap between walls, dodge spikes and saws, and climb as high as you can.

**Full description**
Wall Hop is a fast one-thumb arcade climber.

Your little cube clings to the wall of a neon tower that never ends. Tap anywhere to leap to the other wall. Spikes line the walls, saw blades spin in the middle, and the climb keeps getting faster. How high can you get?

• One-tap controls that anyone can pick up in seconds
• Endless climb that speeds up the higher you go
• Spikes, spinning saws, and sweeping saws to dodge
• Grab coins to unlock nine skins, from Ember and Ghost to the rainbow Prism
• Beat your best height and chase a new record every run
• Plays fully offline. No ads, no accounts, no data collected.
