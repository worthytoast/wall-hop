Wall Hop Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Wall Hop is a single-player game. This page explains what the game does with information about you. The short answer is that it collects nothing.

What the game stores

The game saves your best height, your coin total, the skins you have unlocked, and your sound setting. This information stays on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for an account, a name, or an email address.
  • It does not use the internet. The whole game runs on your device.
  • It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no in-app purchases.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

Permissions

The Android app asks for one permission, vibration, so your phone can buzz when you crash.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project page at github.com/worthytoast/wall-hop.
